# Hitbox Viewer

Draws the hitboxes of everything in a level so you can see exactly what is touching what.

## How to use

Press **Left Alt** to drop the menu down from the top of the screen. Press it again (or click the X) to roll it back up.

In the menu you can switch each type on or off:

- **Show hitboxes** - master switch
- **Solids** (blue) - blocks and slopes
- **Hazards** (red) - spikes, saws and other things that kill you
- **Specials** (green) - portals, pads, orbs and other triggers you touch
- **Player** (yellow) - your icon's hitbox, plus an approximate inner box

The slider controls how see-through the boxes are. Outline thickness is in the mod settings.

## Notes

- Rotated objects are drawn using their bounding box.
- Works on any Geometry Dash version that Geode supports.
